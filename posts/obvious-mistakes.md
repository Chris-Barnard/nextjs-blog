In his book "The Mental Game of Trading", Jarend Tendler ([@JaredTendler](https://x.com/jaredtendler)) writes about the different kinds of mistakes that you can make.
He uses the classification of your A game, B game and C game.
When your performing at your A game level, any mistakes you make are caused by something technical; it's some piece of knowledge which you haven't acquired yet.
In contrast, when you're performing at your C game level, you are making mistakes that are you know are wrong, and you know it seconds after you make them.
It is these mistakes that I want to talk about today.

# Why do we make mistakes that we know are wrong?

Why on earth would I make a mistake that I know to be a mistake seconds after taking the action?
I would argue that we make these mistakes because our thought process has been hijacked by emotional volatility.
